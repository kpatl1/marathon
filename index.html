The Drinking Protocol (Lolla, Mochakk, North Coast, bday, Dom Dolla…)

  • Before going out: full dinner with carbs + protein, 500 ml electrolyte drink.
  • During: 1 water per drink (non-negotiable — set a phone reminder), avoid sugary mixers late night.
  • Before bed: 500–750 ml water + electrolytes, salty snack.
  • Next morning: electrolytes first, salty carb-heavy breakfast (eggs + toast + fruit), 30–45 min zone-1 walk — not a run. Your HRV will be suppressed 24–48 hrs; the plan already schedules rest/easy on every morning-after.
  • Rule: no hard workout within 36 hrs after a heavy night. Long runs are placed BEFORE party weekends, never the morning after.
  • Oct 17 = last big night. After the Oct 16–17 weekend, alcohol costs you real race time (sleep quality, glycogen storage, plasma volume). Two weeks clean before Nov 1.

NYC Course Notes

  • Verrazzano (mi 1–2): biggest climb of the day — go 20–30 s/mi slower than goal. Everyone banks time here and pays at mile 22.
  • Brooklyn (mi 3–13): settle into 9:05–9:10, run the tangents, don't surge with the crowd energy.
  • Queensboro Bridge (mi 15–16): silent, uphill, brutal. Effort stays even, pace drifts — fine.
  • First Ave (mi 16–18): the wall of noise makes everyone sprint. Don't.
  • Fifth Ave grind (mi 22–24): long false-flat uphill. This is what all those Bulgarian split squats were for.
  • Central Park (mi 24+): rolling. Empty the tank from Columbus Circle.
