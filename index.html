Injury early-warning (act on these, don't push through)

Sharp shin pain that persists into walking, achilles stiffness lasting past the first mile, knee pain that worsens during a run → cut the run, take 2 days off, cross-train (bike/swim). Coming off a low run base into 13 weeks of building, calves/shins are the risk zone — that's why calf raises are in every leg day and mileage builds gradually with cutback weeks.

Tightness vs. bone pain — learn the difference, it decides everything: tightness or a diffuse ache that loosens as you warm up is muscle and tendon. Annoying, trainable around, responds to eccentric calf work and ankle mobility. Pain you can pinpoint with one fingertip on the shin bone, or that sharpens the longer you run, is bone stress — that one means stop running and get it looked at. The warmup is your daily test.

NYC Course Notes

  • Verrazzano (mi 1–2): biggest climb of the day — go 20–30 s/mi slower than goal. Everyone banks time here and pays at mile 22.
  • Brooklyn (mi 3–13): settle into 9:05–9:10, run the tangents, don't surge with the crowd energy.
  • Queensboro Bridge (mi 15–16): silent, uphill, brutal. Effort stays even, pace drifts — fine.
  • First Ave (mi 16–18): the wall of noise makes everyone sprint. Don't.
  • Fifth Ave grind (mi 22–24): long false-flat uphill. This is what all those Bulgarian split squats were for.
  • Central Park (mi 24+): rolling. Empty the tank from Columbus Circle.
